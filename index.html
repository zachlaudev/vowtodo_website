A spreadsheet can hold your plans. It can't hold your wedding.

Sheets are great at numbers. Weddings are numbers, people, deadlines and a thousand tiny decisions.

The spreadsheet way

  • One person updates everything
  • Nothing reminds anyone of anything
  • RSVPs chased by text, retyped by hand
  • Seating drawn on paper, redrawn nightly

The VowToDo way

  • The plan keeps itself up to date
  • Reminders go out on their own
  • Guests RSVP by link — tallied for you
  • Seating is drag, drop, done

And other planner apps? Most are a checklist for one person, with the useful parts behind a subscription. VowToDo is the whole wedding — free.
