What “free” does not mean here

  • No ads in the guest list.
  • No marketplace of vendors waiting on the next tap.
  • No guest cap that opens a checkout.
  • No fee for your mum to join.

If you paid before

  • The app used to sell subscriptions and a lifetime unlock on the App Store.
  • That is over. You do not owe another payment to keep using it.
  • Cancel a leftover subscription in your Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Refunds, where they exist, go through Apple.

If we ever charge for something new, it will be optional, written down, and shown before anyone pays. What is free for a wedding you have already started will not quietly move behind a lock. The longer version is in the terms.
