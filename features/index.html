Features

Built for more than one person

The list, the money, the people, and the day. Open it on your phone. The same wedding is in the browser when you want a bigger screen.

Home

The wedding, at a glance.

Days to go. How much of the list is done. What the budget adds up to. How many guests are coming, and how many people are helping. Invite a partner, parents, or friends when you want them — they join free, and the home screen keeps the count. A broadcast sits there for the thing everyone needs to hear once.

Checklist

Grouped by when it is due.

Start from a timeline shaped by your venue and your traditions, or from a blank page. Tasks gather from a year out to the day itself. Each one can hold a date, a category, a cost, and the person who owns it.

Budget

Planned, paid, and left.

Amounts live on the tasks, then roll into one total. You see what is paid and what is still to come, in whatever currency you spend. Couple-only, unless you choose otherwise.

Guests and RSVPs

Replies that file themselves.

Share a link. Guests answer in a browser — no app, no login. Going, can't, and pending stay in one list, with the questions you wrote: a meal, a shuttle, a seat.

The seating chart is that list on a floor plan. Round tables or a long table, dragged to where they sit. It is roomiest on a laptop, which is why the same wedding is in the web app.

The day

A clock you can rearrange.

Tap an empty slot, or hold a block and drag it. The legal wedding and the celebration can be two different days. Everyone with the wedding sees the same times.

See it with your own list

Completely free — every feature, and everyone you invite. iPhone, or the browser.
