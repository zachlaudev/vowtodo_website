The day

The Wedding Day, Hour by Hour

VowToDo · 27 July 2026 · 7 min

A Singapore Chinese wedding is two events in one day. A customary morning across two homes, and a formal dinner. The couples who enjoy it padded the schedule and handed the jobs to other people. This is a realistic template for a dinner banquet. Lunch weddings shift the whole evening block earlier.

The morning

TimeWhat is happening
5:30amBride’s makeup and hair. Photographer and videographer at her home.
7:30amGroom and brothers leave in the bridal car. Have the toll ang bao ready.
8:00amGatecrash. Door games, run by the sisters. Plan 45 to 60 minutes. It runs long.
9:00amHe “finds” her. Veil, a quiet moment, family photographs.
9:30amTea ceremony at the bride’s home. Some families do the groom’s side first.
10:30amTravel to the groom’s home. She is formally welcomed.
11:00amTea ceremony there, then a simple family lunch.
1:00pmRest. Protect this block. You have been up since 5.

The evening banquet

TimeWhat is happening
5:00pmCouple at the venue. Sound check. A run-through with the emcee and the banquet manager.
6:30pmGuests arrive. Sign-in. Ang bao box guarded. A photo montage if you have one.
7:30pmDoors, guests seated. First march-in. First dish.
8:30pmSpeeches between courses. A same-day edit of the morning, if your videographer offers it.
9:15pmSecond march-in, cake, and the yam seng. Three rounds.
9:45pmTable-to-table toasts and photographs.
10:30pmSend-off at the door. Count the ang bao box with two trusted people before you leave.

Roles to assign before the week

  • Best man and brothers: the car, the gatecrash negotiation, the groom’s bag.
  • Sisters: door games, tea logistics, the bride’s kit (heels, safety pins, lipstick).
  • Ang bao guardians: two people at the desk. The box is never alone.
  • Emcee: a friend or a professional. They need the run sheet and the name pronunciations days before, not in the car.
  • A wrangler on each side: someone who knows every uncle and can gather them for photographs.
  • A vendor contact who is not you. Someone else answers the phone.

Three rules that save the day

  • Add 15 minutes between blocks. Gatecrash and tea overrun. They always do.
  • Someone is assigned to make sure the couple eats.
  • The final schedule goes to everyone with a job, not only the wedding party.

Give everyone the same times.
