Frequently asked questions

Everything you might be wondering before you start.

Is VowToDo free?

Anyone you invite joins free. Creating your own wedding space uses a subscription (monthly or yearly, each with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Do my guests need to download the app?

No. Guests reply to your RSVP from any web browser using a link you share — their answers land on your dashboard automatically.

Can we plan together as a couple or family?

Yes. Invite your partner, family and friends, and assign roles — admins can edit everything, while crew see just what they need.

Is there an Android version?

VowToDo is available on iPhone now, and an Android version is on the way. Pop back to this page for the Google Play link when it launches.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes — manage or cancel anytime from your Apple App Store account settings. If you cancel during the free trial you won’t be charged.
