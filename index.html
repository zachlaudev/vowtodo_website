A few questions

What is VowToDo?
A wedding planner app for the checklist, the budget, the guest list, and the day-of schedule. On iPhone and on the web.
Can we plan a wedding with a partner?
Yes. Invite your partner, parents, or a planner. Each person sees the tasks that belong to them.
Does it replace a spreadsheet?
It is an alternative to wedding spreadsheets. Reminders, RSVPs, and seating are built in, not another tab to maintain.
Do guests need the app to RSVP?
No. Share a link. Replies land on your guest list and RSVP tracker.
