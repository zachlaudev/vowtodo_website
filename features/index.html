Features

What the app actually does.

A shared place for the couple, and for whoever is helping. The list, the money, the people, and the day.

01

Checklist

Start from a timeline or from a blank page. Tasks group themselves by when they are due, from a year out to the morning of. Each one can carry a category, a date, a photo, and a name.

Swipe to duplicate a task. Drag to reorder when the hotel changes the cocktail hour. The ROM filing, the tasting, and “buy a second pair of heels” can all live here without becoming a colour-coded mess.

02

Budget

Every task can hold an amount. Those amounts roll into one total: planned, paid, and what is left. You see the banquet and the small things in the same place, in Singapore dollars.

By default only the couple sees it. Admins run the plan. Crew get their jobs. You can open a single task to more people without opening the whole budget.

03

Guests and RSVPs

One list: which side, coming or not, meal, and the note about the uncle who cannot sit with his brother. Share a link. Guests answer in a browser. They do not install anything and they do not make an account.

You write the questions. Shuttle from the MRT, lunch versus dinner, vegetarian, which of the two ceremonies they will actually attend. Replies land on a colour-coded matrix so you can see the holes.

04

Seating chart

Lay the room out. Round tables or long tables, dragged where they actually sit in the ballroom. Then drag guests onto seats. A swap is a drag, not a rewrite of column H.

It is roomiest on a laptop, which is why the same wedding is at vowtodo.com/app. The chart is on its way to the phone as well. Until then, do this part at a desk.

05

Run sheet

The day, line by line. Drag a block when the gatecrash runs long, because it will. Assign each line to the person running it: brothers on the bridal car, sisters on the door games, two people who never leave the ang bao box.

Everyone with access sees the same times. You are not the 6am “what time again” hotline.

06 · Also

The parts that keep other people useful.

07

Crew and roles

Invite with a six-character code. Couple, Admin, Crew. As many people as you need. All of them free. No crew yet? The collaborative screens stay quiet.

08

Visibility

Each task can be private, visible to the people assigned, visible to admins, or visible to everyone in the wedding. The budget is not a group document unless you make it one.

09

Announcements

One post. The people planning with you all see it. Use it when the solemnisation venue changes, or when the bus time does.

10

Reminders

A due date can nudge the owner. You are not copying the same sentence into three family chats.

11

Phone and laptop

The iPhone app, and the same wedding in a browser at vowtodo.com/app. Android is on the way. A browser covers it until then.
