What a Wedding Banquet Really Costs in Singapore

VowToDo · 27 July 2026 · 7 min

The banquet is the largest line in most Singapore wedding budgets, often larger than everything else together. Here is what a table costs in 2026, what the quoted price leaves out, and the mistake that adds empty seats.

Per table, in 2026

Prices below are for a table of 10, before service charge and GST.

VenueTypical price per table
Chinese restaurants and budget hotelsS$500–1,200
Mid-tier hotelsS$1,200–1,800
Luxury hotelsS$1,800–2,600+

Most couples land in the middle band. The brochure price is usually before 10% service charge and 9% GST. Add about 20% to whatever is printed.

A worked example

Two hundred guests is 20 tables. At S$1,500 a table that is S$30,000, and about S$36,000 after service charge and GST. The gown, the photographer, the flowers and the honeymoon are not in that number.

Minimum tables

Ballrooms often require 15 to 25 tables. A guest list of 120 people, against a 20-table minimum, means you pay for seats you will not fill. Match the minimum to the guest count you can confirm, not the count you hope your parents will trim. This is the expensive mistake.

Paying less for the same night

  • Move off Saturday night. Weekday dinners and Sunday lunches often run S$100 to S$300 less per table. On 20 tables that is S$2,000 to S$6,000.
  • Ask for perks, not a lower rate. Hotels rarely cut the per-table price. They will add beer, waive corkage, print more cards, or improve the suite.
  • Read the package as a contract. Tasting, wine per table, corkage, a day-use room, parking, AV. If it is not written down, it is not included.
  • Know the deposit schedule before you sign, including what is refundable if the date moves.

Ang bao helps. It does not pay the bill.

Guests give roughly enough to cover their seat. See the ang bao guide for current ranges. A full room claws back a real share of the banquet. Plan as if it covers part of the night, not the night. Treating ang bao as guaranteed income is how the rest of the budget breaks.
