Is VowToDo really free?

Yes — every feature, every guest, every helper. No trial clock, no card, no locked tiers. If you subscribed before the app went free, there's nothing more to pay; you can manage or cancel any old subscription in your App Store account settings.

How do I invite my partner or helpers?

In the app, open Crew and share your six-character invite code. They download VowToDo (or open the web app), create a free account, and choose “Join a space.”

Do my guests need the app to RSVP?

No. Share your RSVP link and guests reply in any browser — replies land on your dashboard automatically.

Is there an Android app?

It's on the way. Until then, Android users can use the full web app in their browser.

How do I delete my account?

In the mobile app: Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your data. Questions first? Email us.
