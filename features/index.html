Features

What the app actually holds.

A shared book for the couple, and for whoever is helping. The list, the money, the people, and the day. A courthouse morning, a long service, a dinner, a backyard.

01

Checklist

Start from a timeline, or from a blank page. Tasks gather by when they are due, from a year out to the morning of. Each one can hold a date, a name, a note, and a photograph.

02

Budget

Every task can hold an amount. Those amounts become one total: planned, paid, and what is left. The venue and the small things sit together, in whatever currency you spend.

By default only the couple sees it. Admins can run the plan. Crew get their jobs. You can open a single task without opening the whole budget.

03

Guests and RSVPs

One list: which side, coming or not, the meal, and the note about the uncle who cannot sit with his brother. Share a link. They reply in a browser. No app, no account.

04

Seating

Lay the room out. Round tables or a long table, dragged to where they actually sit. Then drag a guest onto a seat. A swap is a drag, not a rewrite of a column.

It is roomiest on a laptop, which is why the same wedding is at vowtodo.com/app.

05

The run of the day

Line by line. Drag a block when the photos run long, because they will. Put a name on each line: someone on the car, someone on the grandparents, two people who do not leave the gifts.

Everyone with the wedding sees the same times. You are not the 6am hotline.
