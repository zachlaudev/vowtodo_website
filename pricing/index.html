Pricing

Free.
Actually free.

Every feature, every guest, every person you invite. On iPhone, and in the browser. No trial clock and no card.

  • Checklist, reminders, photos on tasks free
  • Budget, visible to the couple free
  • Guest list, RSVP links, seating chart free
  • Wedding-day run sheet free
  • Announcements and helpers free
  • Web app for the laptop work free

The app used to sell a subscription and a lifetime unlock on the App Store. That is over. If an old subscription is still renewing, cancel it in your Apple account — deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds, where they exist, go through Apple.

If we ever charge for something new, it will be optional and shown before anyone pays. What is free for a wedding you have already started will not quietly move behind a lock. The longer version is in the terms.
