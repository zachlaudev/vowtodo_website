The shelf

Notes for planning a wedding.

The essays on this shelf are written for weddings in Singapore: the registry, the banquet, ang bao, and the customs around a Chinese wedding. The app itself is for any wedding. More notes, for other places, will follow.

01 Planning

The month-by-month wedding checklist

What to do 12 months out, and what can wait until the RSVPs are actually in. Written for a Singapore calendar: ROM, the ballroom, Guo Da Li.

8 min 02 Budget

What a banquet really costs

2026 per-table prices for restaurants and hotels, the minimum-table trap, and the 20% the brochure leaves off.

7 min 03 Legal

ROM: registering your marriage

The 21-day rule, the fees, two witnesses, and solemnising somewhere that is not the banquet hall.

6 min 04 Traditions

Chinese wedding customs, in practice

Guo Da Li, Si Dian Jin, An Chuang, gatecrashing, and the tea ceremony. What they are, and how couples shorten them.

9 min 05 Etiquette

Ang bao rates in 2026

How much to give by venue and meal, plus the rules about 4s, pairs, and not attending.

6 min 06 The day

The wedding day, hour by hour

From the 5:30am makeup call to the yam seng, with the helper roles to assign before anyone is tired.
