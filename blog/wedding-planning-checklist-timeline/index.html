The Wedding Planning Checklist: A Month-by-Month Timeline

By the VowToDo team · 6 July 2026 · 7 min read

The single biggest reason wedding planning feels overwhelming isn’t the number of tasks — it’s not knowing what to do when. Here’s a clear month-by-month timeline you can follow from the moment you’re engaged to the morning of the big day.

12+ months before

This is the foundation. Get these right and everything else falls into place.

  • Agree a rough budget and who’s contributing.
  • Draft a guest-list estimate — it drives venue size and cost.
  • Pick a season or a shortlist of dates.
  • Tour and book your ceremony and reception venues.
  • Book the highest-demand vendors early: photographer, caterer, band or DJ.

9–11 months before

  • Lock the date once the venue is confirmed.
  • Send save-the-dates so guests can plan travel.
  • Start shopping for wedding attire — alterations take time.
  • Book officiant, florist, and any rentals.

6–8 months before

  • Finalise the guest list and gather addresses.
  • Plan the honeymoon and check passport validity.
  • Arrange transport and any room blocks for out-of-town guests.
  • Order invitations and decide how you’ll collect RSVPs.

4–5 months before

  • Confirm the menu and schedule a tasting.
  • Choose the cake and finalise flowers.
  • Buy wedding bands.
  • Draft the day-of timeline and share it with key vendors.

2–3 months before

  • Send invitations (aim for RSVPs six to eight weeks before).
  • Finalise the ceremony order and any readings or vows.
  • Confirm numbers with the caterer as replies arrive.
  • Start a seating plan.

The final month

  • Chase any missing RSVPs and lock the final headcount.
  • Give the venue and caterer final numbers and dietary needs.
  • Confirm timings with every vendor in writing.
  • Assign day-of roles to your crew so you’re not fielding questions.

The week of, and the day itself

  • Pack an emergency kit and lay out everything the night before.
  • Share the minute-by-minute schedule with your wedding party.
  • Delegate — hand off point-of-contact duties so you can be present.
  • Then breathe. The planning is done; enjoy the day you built.
