ROM in Singapore: How to Register Your Marriage

VowToDo · 27 July 2026 · 6 min

Before the gown and the yam seng, one thing makes you married: solemnisation through the Registry of Marriages. The steps are shorter than most couples expect. The deadlines are not flexible, so they go at the top of the list.

The rule that matters: 21 days

You file a Notice of Marriage at least 21 days before the solemnisation date. Filing is online, through Our Marriage Journey at marriage.gov.sg, with Singpass. Popular dates and popular solemnisers go early. Do not leave the notice for the week you are also chasing RSVPs.

At least one of you is a Singapore citizen or PRS$42

The steps

  • Pick the date and the room. You can solemnise at ROM, or somewhere else: a restaurant, the banquet venue, a garden, your living room. Anywhere else needs a licensed solemniser.
  • File the notice online and pay the fee.
  • Finish verification and the statutory declaration. Both of you confirm your documents and declare that you are free to marry.
  • Confirm the solemniser if you are not at ROM. Invite a licensed solemniser and upload their consent. A small gift, commonly S$80 to S$200, is the usual thanks.
  • Two witnesses. Aged 21 or over, with valid identification, in the room. Not on a video call.
  • Solemnise. The ceremony is often 15 to 30 minutes: declarations, and signing the certificate.

Documents to have ready

  • NRIC for citizens and PRs, or a passport for foreigners.
  • If either of you was married before: the divorce or annulment papers, or a former spouse’s death certificate.
  • A deed poll, if a name has changed.
  • Parental consent if either of you is 18, 19 or 20.

ROM first, banquet later

A lot of Singapore couples split the two. A small solemnisation with immediate family, then the customary wedding and the banquet months later, sometimes a year later. Nothing forbids it. Decide early. It changes the gown, the guest list, and which date the 21 days are counted from.

Muslim marriages go through the Registry of Muslim Marriages (ROMM), with its own requirements. Start at marriage.gov.sg.

Keep the legal dates where your partner can see them.

Put the filing, the solemniser and the two witnesses on the checklist, with the real deadline, not “sometime before the banquet”.
