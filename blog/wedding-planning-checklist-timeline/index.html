The Wedding Planning Checklist: A Month-by-Month Timeline

VowToDo · 6 July 2026 · 8 min

The hard part is rarely the number of tasks. It is not knowing which month they belong to. This is a Singapore calendar: the ballroom, the ROM notice, the customary morning and the banquet are different deadlines.

12 months before, and earlier

Get the constraints down before you fall in love with a ballroom.

  • Agree a budget, and who is paying into it. Parents often are. Write the number down before the first hotel tour.
  • Estimate the guest list. In Singapore the list sets the table count, and the table count decides whether a ballroom will take you.
  • Pick a season, then a shortlist of dates. If your families use auspicious dates, those go on the shortlist now. They book out.
  • Tour ballrooms and restaurants. Saturday dinners go first.
  • Book photography and videography. The people you want share a calendar with those dates.

9 to 11 months before

  • Lock the date once the venue confirms it in writing.
  • Decide whether ROM and the banquet are the same day. Plenty of couples solemnise first and banquet months later. That choice rewrites the rest of this list.
  • Send save-the-dates, especially to anyone flying in.
  • Start attire. Gown, suit, qun kua, alterations. It takes longer than the shop says.
  • Book a licensed solemniser if you are not marrying at the ROM building.

6 to 8 months before

  • Finish the guest list and collect addresses. This part is easier on a laptop.
  • Talk to both families about Guo Da Li: what is given, what is returned, who is in the room.
  • Plan the honeymoon and check passports.
  • Decide how RSVPs come back. A link is kinder than a WhatsApp thread you have to retype.

4 to 5 months before

  • Do the menu tasting. Ask what the per-table price becomes after the 10% service charge and 9% GST.
  • Wedding bands. Flowers. A cake, if you are having one.
  • Draft the day-of run sheet: gatecrash, tea, a real rest, march-in, yam seng. Send a rough version to the emcee.
  • Name two ang bao guardians. Not you.

2 to 3 months before

  • Send invitations. Ask for replies six to eight weeks out.
  • File the Notice of Marriage at least 21 days before solemnisation, online, with Singpass. The deadline is the solemnisation, which may not be the banquet.
  • Line up two witnesses, 21 or older, with identification, physically in the room.
  • Start seating once the replies are real. Match the count to the venue’s minimum tables, not to the list you hoped for.

The last month

  • Chase the missing RSVPs and lock the headcount.
  • Send final numbers and dietary needs to the venue.
  • Confirm every vendor’s call time in writing.
  • Hand out day-of roles. You should not be the person holding the teapot and the questions.

The week of, and the morning

  • Pack a kit: safety pins, plasters, lipstick, cash for the door games, and a printed list of kinship titles for the tea ceremony.
  • Share the minute-by-minute schedule with everyone who has a job.
  • Protect the afternoon rest. Makeup starts around 5:30am.
  • Then stop adding tasks. The list has done its work.

Move anything that does not fit your families. The order is the useful part, not the superstition of doing it in this exact sequence.
