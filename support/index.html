Is VowToDo really free?

Yes. Every feature, every guest, every helper. No trial clock, no card, no locked tiers. If you subscribed before the app went free, there is nothing more to pay. Manage or cancel an old subscription in your App Store account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I invite my partner or helpers?

In the app, open Crew and share your six-character invite code. They download VowToDo, or open the web app, create a free account, and choose “Join a space.”

Do my guests need the app to RSVP?

No. Share the RSVP link. Guests reply in any browser. The answers show up on your list.

Is there an Android app?

It is on the way. Until then, the full planner is in the web app.

How do I delete my account?

In the mobile app: Me, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your data. If you want to ask something first, email us.
